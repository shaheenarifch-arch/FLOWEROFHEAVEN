Questions

Who delivers the flowers?

Your order is sent through our partner Florist One to a local florist near the recipient, who arranges and hand-delivers the bouquet.

Which days do you deliver?

Monday to Saturday. There is no Sunday delivery. Same-day orders must be placed before 1 PM in the recipient's time zone. Holiday cut-offs (such as Valentine's Day and Mother's Day) can be earlier.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Your card goes straight to Authorize.net, the card processor Florist One uses. Your card number never touches our website or servers.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Contact Florist One customer service at 888-610-8262 (or text 501-406-0358) within three days of delivery, with your order number. Resolutions can include a replacement, a refund or a partial credit. See their customer service guarantee.

Can I send flowers to a funeral home or hospital?

Yes. At checkout, add the facility name in the "Funeral home, hospital or business" field. Our Sympathy occasion has arrangements made for services.
