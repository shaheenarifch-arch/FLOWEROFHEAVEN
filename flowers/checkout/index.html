Delivery details & payment

Who gets the flowers

10 digits. The florist only calls if they can't find the address or nobody is home.

Delivery date & card

· Printed on the card that comes with the flowers. Add your name if you want them to know who sent it.

Delivery is Monday to Saturday (no Sunday delivery), usually 9 AM to 7 PM. Same-day orders must be placed before 1 PM in the recipient's time zone, and holiday cut-offs can be earlier. Florist One delivery and guarantee details.

Your details (for the receipt)

Use the address your card statement goes to.

Payment

3 digits on the back, or 4 on the front for American Express.

🔒Your card details go straight to Authorize.net, the payment processor Florist One uses. They never reach our website or servers. Florist One charges your card and appears on your statement.
