Questions

How do I buy from Flower of Heaven?

Click Buy on Amazon on any product. You check out on Amazon.com, where the item is sold by Love$Care Store. Amazon takes the payment and shows delivery and return details before you order.

Are prices on this site the final price?

No. Prices here were checked in November 2026 and can change. Amazon always shows the current price, shipping and tax at checkout.

How do returns work?

Returns go through Amazon. Open Your Orders on Amazon, choose the item and select Return or replace items. Eligible orders are also covered by the Amazon A-to-z Guarantee.

Do you ship outside the US?

Our listings are on Amazon.com for US delivery. Amazon shows whether an item can ship to your address at checkout.
