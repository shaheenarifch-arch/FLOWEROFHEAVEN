Feminized vs. autoflower in one minute

Frequently asked questions

What are the best cannabis seeds for beginners?

Feminized or autoflowering seeds of forgiving, well-known strains are the usual starting point, because there are no male plants to remove and autoflowers finish quickly. Blimburn Seeds keeps a dedicated best-for-beginners list.

What is the difference between feminized and autoflowering seeds?

Feminized seeds produce only female plants. Autoflowering seeds flower based on age rather than light cycle, so they finish faster and stay smaller. Many seeds are both feminized and autoflowering.
