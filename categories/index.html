Frequently asked questions

Which seed category should I choose first?

Start with seed type (feminized or autoflowering), then narrow by goal: high THC, CBD, high yield or beginner-friendly.

What are F1 seeds?

F1 seeds come from crossing two stable parent lines; breeders use them for uniformity and hybrid vigor.
